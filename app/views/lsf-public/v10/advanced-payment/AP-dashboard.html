What is an advance payment?

If you need money in advance to pay for travel and accommodation (TDAE) costs, you can request an advance payment rather than wait to be reimbursed.

This is not a loan, grant or extra payment. It simply means you receive money before you pay for travel or accommodation, instead of claiming it back afterwards.

How much can you request?

The amount you can request depends on your previous TDAE claims:

  • £200 if any previous claims were for travel only
  • £400 if any previous claims were for accommodation only
  • Up to £600 if any previous claims included travel and accommodation

You can use your advance payment for any eligible travel or accommodation costs.

How does it work?

  1. Request an advance payment.
  2. Receive your payment 10 working days later.
  3. Pay for your travel or accommodation.
  4. Submit your TDAE claim with receipts or evidence.
  5. Your approved claim reduces your advance payment balance.

You can request another advance payment when your remaining balance is £50 or less.

Example

  • You receive a £200 advance payment.
  • You submit a TDAE claim for £100 of eligible travel costs.
  • Your remaining balance is £100.

If you do not use all of your advance payment

You are responsible for repaying any unused advance payment

If you do not use all of your advance payment for eligible costs, you must repay the unused amount.

Where possible, this will be deducted from your final Term 3 Training Grant payment.

If this is not possible, you will need to repay the remaining balance.

Example

  • You receive £400
  • You use £250 for eligible costs.
  • The remaining £150 will be taken from your final Term 3 Training Grant payment or paid by you.

If you leave your course early

If you leave your course and have any unused advance payment, you will need to repay the remaining amount.

We will provide information about how to repay any money owed.

Before you request an advance payment

Make sure you understand that:

  • it helps you pay costs before claiming through TDAE
  • You must keep receipts and provide evidence
  • it can only be used for eligible travel and accommodation costs
  • unused money will need to be repaid
  • you can request another payment when your balance is £50 or less

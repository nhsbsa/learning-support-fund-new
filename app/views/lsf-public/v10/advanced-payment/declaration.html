Advanced payment declaration

Important: Declaration

I understand that the advance payment I am receiving is in respect of my anticipated Travel Costs, Dual Accommodation Costs, or both, under the Learning Support Fund (LSF).

I understand and accept that:

The advance payment is based on my expected eligibility and costs.

I must continue to submit eligible Travel and/or Dual Accommodation claims in line with Learning Support Fund requirements while receiving this advance payment.

Any approved claims I submit will be used to offset the advance payment received.

I must inform NHSBSA Student Services immediately of any changes in my circumstances that may affect my entitlement to this support. If I withdraw, abandon, suspend, defer or interrupt the course permanently or temporarily for any reason, regardless of whether I intend to return.

If any of the advance payment remains unused, it may be deducted from your final year Term 3 Learning Support Fund payment.

I understand that if I withhold information, fail to report changes in my circumstances, or provide false, inaccurate, or misleading information:

My financial support may be reduced, withdrawn, or stopped.

I may be required to repay all or part of the advance payment.

Future applications for financial support may be affected.

Disciplinary action may be taken where appropriate.

I may be liable to criminal prosecution and/or civil proceedings.

By ticking this box, I confirm that I have read, understood, and agree to the terms of this declaration.

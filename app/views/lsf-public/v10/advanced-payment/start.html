Request advance payments for travel and accommodation costs

What is an advance payment?

If you need money in advance to pay for travel and accommodation costs, you can request an advance payment rather than wait to be reimbursed.

This is not a loan, grant or extra payment. It simply means you receive money before you pay for travel or accommodation, instead of claiming it back afterwards.

How much can I request?

The amount you can request depends on your previous TDAE claims. You can request:

  • £200 if your previous claims were for travel only
  • £400 if your previous claims were for accommodation only
  • Up to £600 if your previous claims included travel and accommodation

You can use your advance payment for any eligible travel or accommodation costs.

How does it work?

You’ll need to:

  • request an advance payment
  • receive payment into your bank account 10 working days later
  • pay for your travel or accommodation
  • submit your TDAE claims as usual with receipts or evidence

When your TDAE claims are approved, the approved amount reduces the amount of your advance payment you have left.

How do I keep track of my advance payment?

Once your advance payment is in your bank account, you may find it difficult to keep track of how much you have remaining.

We work out for you how much of your advance payment you have left based on the TDAE claims you submit and have approved. You can view this information and your TDAE claims online.

Your advance payment page will show:

  • how much advance payment you received
  • which TDAE claims have been approved
  • how much was taken from your advance payment for each approved claim
  • how much advance payment you have remaining

If you have spent some of your advance payment but have not yet submitted a TDAE claim, this will still show as money you have remaining. To help you keep track, it’s important to submit TDAE claims as soon as you can.

You can request more than one advance payment

You can request advance payments more than once during your course.

Once you have £50 or less remaining from your current advance payment, you can request another payment.

What happens if I do not use all of my advance payment?

If you do not use all of your advance payment, we’ll deduct any remaining advance payment from your final Term 3 Training Grant payment.

For example:

  • you receive £400
  • you use £250 for eligible costs
  • the remaining £150 will be deducted from your final Term 3 Training Grant payment if it has not been used by the deadline

If you leave your course early

If you leave your course early and have not used all of your advance payment, you will need to pay the remaining amount.

We will provide information about how to repay any money owed.

You do not need to decide now

You can request an advance payment at any time during your course.

Request advance payments for travel and accommodation costs

What is an advance payment?

If you need money in advance to pay for travel and accommodation (TDAE) costs, you can request an advance payment rather than wait to be reimbursed.

This is not a loan, grant or extra payment. It simply means you receive money before you pay for travel or accommodation, instead of claiming it back afterwards.

How much can you request?

The amount you can request depends on your previous TDAE claims:

  • £200 if any previous claims were for travel only.
  • £400 if any previous claims were for accommodation only.
  • Up to £600 if any previous claims included travel and accommodation.

You can use your advance payment for any eligible travel or accommodation costs.

How does it work?

  1. Request an advance payment.
  2. Receive your payment 10 working days later.
  3. Pay for your travel or accommodation.
  4. Submit your TDAE claim with receipts or evidence.
  5. Approved claims are taken from your advance payment balance.

You can request another advance payment when your remaining balance is £50 or less.

Things you need to consider

If you’re thinking of requesting a TDAE advance payment for the first time, there are a few things you need to be aware of.

Unused advance payments

If you do not use all of your advance payment, we’ll deduct the remaining balance from your final Term 3 Training Grant payment.

For example:

  • you receive an advance payment of £400
  • you use £250 for travel and accommodation
  • the remaining £150 will be taken from your final Term 3 Training Grant payment

If you leave your course early

If you leave your course and have any unused advance payment, you will need to repay the remaining amount.

We’ll provide information on how to repay any money owed.

You do not need to decide now

You can request an advance payment at any time during your course.
